# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, respectful, and welcoming environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and polite.
- Listen to different opinions.
- Use inclusive and appropriate language.
- Help other students and contributors.
- Give constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment or bullying.
- Discriminatory comments.
- Insulting or threatening others.
- Sharing inappropriate content.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe inappropriate behaviour, please contact the repository owner through GitHub.

All reports will be handled respectfully.

## Scope

These rules apply to project discussions, contributions, issues, and pull requests.

## Conclusion

Everyone is expected to help maintain a safe and respectful learning environment.
